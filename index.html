Getting in

Join in three steps

You need Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11. No mods required.

Start 1.21.11

In the Minecraft Launcher, pick an installation set to release 1.21.11 and press Play.

Add the server

Go to Multiplayer, then Add Server, and paste play.unbornsmp.online as the address.

Join carefully

Press Join Server. From this moment your life counts, and there is no second one.

    FAQ

    Questions before you're born

    What happens when I die?

    You become unborn. You can't play on the server again, and your death is added to the record of the unborn.

    What if I died to a bug or lag?

    Open a ticket in the Discord. Staff check the logs for every appeal and can restore a player when the death wasn't fair.

    Which version do I need?

    Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11. Full steps and fixes are on the How to join page.

    Can I use mods?

    Performance and visual mods like Sodium, Iris and shaders are fine. Hacked clients, x-ray and anything that gives an unfair edge get you banned.

    Can I buy an extra life?

    No. Nothing in the store bypasses the one-life rule.

    When does the server open?

    The launch date will be posted in the Discord first.
