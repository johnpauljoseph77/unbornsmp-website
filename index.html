    FAQ

    Quick answers. Ask anything else in the Discord.

    What happens when I die?

    You become unborn. You can no longer play on the server, and your death is added to the record. That is the whole point of the server.

    What if I died to a bug?

    Open a ticket in the Discord. Staff check the logs for every appeal and can restore a player when the death was not fair.

    Which version do I need?

    Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11. Full steps are on the How to join page.

    Can I use mods?

    Performance and visual mods like Sodium, Iris and shaders are fine. Hacked clients, x-ray and anything that gives an unfair edge will get you banned.

    Can I buy an extra life?

    No. Nothing in the store bypasses the one-life rule, and nothing ever will.

    When does it open?

    Launch date will be announced in the Discord first. Join now so you don't miss it.
