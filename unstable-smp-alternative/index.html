If you like watching story-driven SMPs like Unstable SMP and want a server where your own choices matter, Unborn SMP is a public hardcore SMP you can join. You get one life. What you build, who you fight and how you die become part of the server's story.

Why players pick Unborn SMP

Unborn SMP is its own server with its own world and lore. It isn't a copy of any series. It was made for players who want an SMP where the stakes are real.

  1. Every life matters

    Hardcore rules apply to the whole server. When you die you become unborn and can't play again, so every fight and every trip into the Nether is a real risk.

  2. You make the story

    Factions raise armies, take ground and go to war. Nothing is scripted. The server's history is written by the players who live and die in it.

  3. Anyone can join

    There's no application or whitelist. Install Java 1.21.11, add play.unbornsmp.online and you're in.

  4. Fair from day one

    A server-side anticheat watches for hacks, nothing in the store bypasses the one-life rule, and staff can restore a player who died to a bug.

Not affiliated

Unborn SMP is an independent server. It isn't connected to, endorsed by or run by Unstable SMP or its creators. We mention the name only to help players looking for a similar kind of server.

Questions

Is Unborn SMP part of Unstable SMP?

No. Unborn SMP is an independent server with its own world, lore and staff. It isn't affiliated with Unstable SMP.

What makes Unborn SMP different from a normal SMP?

You only get one life. If you die, you don't respawn and your name is added to the record of the unborn. Factions, wars and the whole world's history are shaped by that rule.

Can I join Unborn SMP on Bedrock?

Not right now. Unborn SMP is Java Edition 1.21.11 only.

Is Unborn SMP cracked or anarchy?

Neither. A paid Java account is required, and the server has rules, staff and an anticheat to protect each player's one life.

How do I join?

Use the address play.unbornsmp.online in Minecraft Java 1.21.11. The How to join page has the full steps.
